About me

Hi, I'm Zeno — a passionate cosplayer who loves transforming into beloved characters from anime, games, and beyond. Every costume I create is a labor of love, from initial sketches all the way to the final reveal on the convention floor.

I specialize in handcrafted builds, intricate prop-making, and capturing the essence of each character through pose and expression. Cosplay, to me, is where fandom meets craftsmanship.

Based in the Philippines and active in the local and international cosplay community, I'm always working on my next character.
